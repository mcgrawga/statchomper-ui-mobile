Privacy Policy for HoopTrack

Last updated: January 18, 2026

Introduction

HoopTrack ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

Information We Collect

HoopTrack stores basketball game statistics and player information locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

Data Stored Locally:

  • Player names (first names only, as you choose to enter them)
  • Game statistics (shots made/missed, points, dates)
  • Your Pro version purchase status (managed by Google Play)

How We Use Your Information

All data is stored locally on your device to:

  • Display your game statistics and player performance
  • Track shooting percentages and improvement over time
  • Provide personalized insights based on your recorded games

Data Storage and Security

All game data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. This data:

  • Remains on your device and is not transmitted to any servers
  • Is protected by your device's security measures
  • Will be deleted if you uninstall the app

In-App Purchases

HoopTrack offers a Pro version as a one-time in-app purchase. All payment processing is handled securely by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information. Purchase records are managed by Google Play according to their privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

HoopTrack uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing: For processing in-app purchases. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy
  • Expo/React Native: Development framework. No data is sent to Expo servers in production builds

Children's Privacy

HoopTrack is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. All data remains local to the device.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual players or games within the app
  • Uninstalling the app, which removes all local data

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: support@hooptrackapp.com

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • You have complete control over your data
  • You can export or delete data at any time
  • No data is shared with third parties
  • No data is used for advertising or analytics
